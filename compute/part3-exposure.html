Chapter 03

How to get exposure

The realistic menu, from most direct to most indirect. Not a recommendation to trade — just an honest map of the terrain, and what could go wrong on each route.

Direct — trade the futures themselves most direct
CONTENT → Who can access the contracts once live, margin, contract sizes (whatever is public). Then the two headline risks:
Liquidity risk. A brand-new market may never attract enough participants to trade in and out cleanly.
Basis risk. A cash-settled hedge only protects you if the reference index actually tracks the prices you face — different GPU mix, region, or contract terms and the hedge drifts from reality.
Adjacent — spot marketplaces & leasing semi-direct
CONTENT → Compute Exchange and other spot/OTC platforms, GPU leasing deals — what you'd actually do, barriers, risks.
Indirect — equity proxies most indirect
CONTENT → Chipmakers, neocloud operators, data-center REITs, the exchanges themselves — correlated exposure, and how loose that correlation really is.
