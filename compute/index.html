Summer research · The emerging market for compute futures

GPU time is starting to trade like oil, wheat, and electricity.

In May 2026 two of the world's biggest exchanges announced futures tied to the price of renting a GPU. This site walks through what that market is, how you'd actually get exposure to it, and the genuinely strange math underneath — a commodity you can't store, that gets cheaper as a better chip ships.
