What the physical market actually charges for the same chipindicative detail →

Each bar is one GPU at one rental type, drawn against its own index so the three are comparable. Bars are provider medians, not raw offers — one marketplace lists ~45% of all listings, so an offer-level percentile would describe that venue rather than the market. Reserved and serverless listings are collected but never drawn in either mode. Listings marked unavailable are excluded. Source: gpus.io public catalogue, neo-cloud providers only, matching the index basis; hyperscaler list rates sit far above this range and are out of scope.
