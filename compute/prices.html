New silicon vs old — the year's real story

The premium for the newest chip kept shrinking: B200 has held at or below its 2.2x training-performance parity since mid-December, while A100 spent the whole year well above its 0.45x parity line.

Generational price ratios vs training-performance parity

Price ratio vs H100 (lines) against MLPerf-derived performance parity (dashed). Below the dashed line = cheap per unit of training compute. The mid-December parity crossing coincides with a Silicon Data index revision — the robust finding is the eight months at/below parity since.

Ratios author-calculated from Silicon Data indices. Parity: MLPerf Training v4.1 (Nov 2024), B200 = 2.0–2.2x H100; MLPerf Inference v3.0 (Apr 2023), H100 ≈ 2.2x A100. B200 inference parity is ~3.5x — against that, B200 is decisively cheap.

What the forward market expects

Six-month forwards sit 7–10% below spot for all three GPUs, after a projected tightness hump two to four months out. Term rates ≈ the average of the forward path: locking capacity costs about what the market expects spot to average anyway — the lock buys certainty, not a discount.
