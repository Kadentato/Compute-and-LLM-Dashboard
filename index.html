Read this first

This is gateway traffic: developer tools, agents, and apps routing through OpenRouter and Vercel AI Gateway. It is a proxy, not the market share of open models overall.

Consumer chat — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude.ai, Grok — never touches a gateway, and no lab publishes token counts. No public source measures "all tokens in the world"; this page doesn't pretend to.

The two gateways disagree because they see different customers: OpenRouter skews cost-sensitive and hobbyist, Vercel skews production apps. The spread between them is a finding, not an error — which is why there is no blended average.

Tokens through each gateway's aggregated "other" bucket and unidentifiable (cloaked pre-release) models are excluded from both lines — the gray band in the composition charts below shows how much that is.

The Vercel line before May 2026 is a floor, not a measurement: Vercel only names each day's top models, and until DeepSeek V4 broke into that list in late April 2026, open-weight usage sat inside the unclassifiable "Other" bucket.
